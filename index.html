Does this receipt work?

Look for vendor name/address, service or purchase date, itemized goods/services and prices, total, and evidence of payment. A card statement alone is insufficient.

General rule: every purchasing-card transaction needs a receipt; personal/shared-liability expenses over $50 need one. Airfare and lodging have additional documentation requirements regardless of amount. Per diem uses the allowance instead of meal receipts.

Missing or foreign receipt?

First request a duplicate from the vendor, particularly for lodging. Use Workday’s missing-receipt process if needed and ask travel support about exceptions. Translate key foreign-language details and document the USD conversion; UW card imports are converted automatically. Remove unnecessary sensitive information from attachments.

Which expenses could you claim?

Up to 20 rows. Keep a row as a single eligible amount that can be claimed in full. Read “What may be eligible?” below before confirming eligibility.

What may be eligible?
  • Conference registration, policy-compliant airfare, business lodging, airport/business ground transport, parking, and baggage may qualify with documentation.
  • Use the official UW lodging and meal calculator for the dates and locations. Deduct provided meals. Do not add separate meal receipts or incidental tips on top of per diem.
  • Business mileage may qualify for authorized drivers; use the rate applicable on the travel date. Do not claim both personal-car mileage and its fuel, or normal commuting.
  • Exclude personal vacation days, companion costs, duplicate/already-paid reimbursement, and unapproved costs. Ask about unusual expenses before checking eligibility.
  • Grant or sponsor terms may be more restrictive. Split funding is not the same as formal sponsored-award cost sharing.

Sources: Conference travel, Ground transport, SYS 405.

Where do I enter these amounts in Workday?
  1. Open the expense line. Keep its full amount equal to the documented transaction amount.
  2. In Itemization, select Add. Enter Pool A’s share in Total Amount, a specific Memo, and the approved A driver worktag. Verify the related Cost Center, Fund and Function.
  3. Add another itemization for Pool B’s share and its approved driver worktag. For zero shares, omit that itemization.
  4. Complete any expense-specific details. Select Done. All itemizations must sum to the expense line total.

If whole expenses belong to different accounts, assign each expense line its own funding instead. Once you pass the initial header, correct funding on each line or itemization. Official itemization instructions ↗

4 · Check & submit

Take one last look

These checks help you review your report. They do not inspect Workday or approve reimbursement.

If Workday shows an error

Red error: open the error summary, go to the affected line, and correct it before submission. Check for a Hold business purpose, missing required details, and itemization totals or worktags.

Orange alert: review the reason and resolve or explain it as appropriate. It may remain for an approver to review.

Click another line or the Header tab after a correction to refresh validation. Workday troubleshooting ↗

After you submit

Submit in Workday and check the report’s status in Expenses Hub. Draft means it has not been submitted. An approved report and a paid report are different stages.

If sent back, read the approver’s comments, fix the report and resubmit. Contact your department assistant for funding and local routing questions.

Know which guidance you are following

Reviewed November 19, 2026. This companion is an independent working aid for COEPS; the linked policies and your authorized approver govern. Confirm the marked local questions before relying on this for a live claim.

Items for COEPS / travel-office review

  • Timing: the Nov. 19 System amendment measures travel/training reimbursement from the end of travel/course. COEPS’s Aug. 27 announcement still asks for advance expenses within 30 days of the transaction. Verify the current local rule; meet the earlier applicable deadline.
  • Business Purpose: the System guide includes Conference, Business Travel and Professional Development. A Nov. 19 local review instruction used Business Travel for one report. That individual instruction is not a rule for everyone. Follow your report’s reviewer; do not leave a Hold selection.
  • Annual awards: use the current award letter and approved funding strings. Faculty dissemination is presenting-only per Nikki’s instructions. Confirm which expenses and dates each award covers.
  • Conference evidence: the agenda/program establishes dates and provided meals. Presentation acceptance or a program page naming you is included for presenting-related funding per Nikki’s requested workflow; confirm the exact local evidence standard.
  • Fiscal close: the June 12, 2026 notice was for FY26 only. Obtain the new year’s cutoff; do not reuse it as an annual deadline.
Why this is designed this way

Adult learning and performance support: start with a real task and let experienced users skip directly to the tool they need. Cognitive load: a short sequence, consistent field labels, worked examples, and help beside the relevant field reduce searching and remembering.

UDL: keyboard-operable buttons, visible labels, text equivalents for every hotspot, optional practice, and printable/downloadable notes provide different ways to access the same task. Choices and immediate actionable feedback support agency. See CAST choice and autonomy and action-oriented feedback.

Faculty development: the optional worked scenario and invitation to review with a department colleague adapt experiential learning, feedback and workplace support described by Steinert et al. (2016). That review concerns teaching development; applying its principles to expense-report support is a design choice, not evidence that this tool has been validated.

Pilot: have a faculty presenter, a faculty attendee and an instructional staff member complete realistic cases with a department assistant. Observe time, missing documents, funding errors and points of confusion. Revise, then name a policy owner to recheck the guide after each Workday or allocation update.
